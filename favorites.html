25 MIN · EASY

Chicken Tacos

Juicy chicken, warm tortillas, and your favorite fresh toppings.

View recipe

Ingredients

chicken, tortillas, tomatoes, onion, lime

  1. Dice the chicken and season with salt, pepper, and taco seasoning.
  2. Cook chicken in a lightly oiled skillet until it reaches 165°F (74°C).
  3. Warm the tortillas, fill with chicken, and top with chopped tomatoes, onion, and lime.

30 MIN · EASY

Chicken Salad

A fresh, satisfying salad for an easy lunch at home.

View recipe

Ingredients

chicken, lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, olive oil

  1. Season the chicken and cook in a skillet until it reaches 165°F (74°C). Let rest, then slice.
  2. Wash and chop the lettuce, tomatoes, and cucumber.
  3. Toss vegetables with olive oil and your favorite dressing. Top with chicken.

45 MIN · EASY

Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Creamy Alfredo sauce, tender chicken, and comforting pasta.

View recipe

Ingredients

chicken, pasta, cream, parmesan, garlic, butter

  1. Cook pasta according to package instructions, reserving some pasta water.
  2. Season chicken and cook until it reaches 165°F (74°C), then slice.
  3. Melt butter in a pan, add minced garlic, then cream. Simmer gently and stir in Parmesan.
  4. Toss with pasta and chicken. Add a splash of pasta water to loosen the sauce.
